Use this notice in the README and applicable eventual content packs/releases. Record source revision, changes of translation origin, attribution and the specific permission evidence with published content.

## Important boundaries

- The permission is a public, project-specific statement covering the **translations** requested for Spoken Ukrainian. It is **not** a general SPDX license for the ClassicUA repository or a sublicense for unrelated material.
- It does not necessarily grant rights to independent sources used for Places/Bosses stories, artwork, fonts, Blizzard assets, other authors' content outside the described translation grant or provider technology. Review each separately.
- TTS generation is permitted by ClassicUA's maintainer for these translations, but selected providers, voice models, storage and distribution may impose their **own** usage terms, privacy conditions or costs.
- Permission does **not** automatically authorize our agent to spend money, send content to an external TTS service, create public releases or change third-party repositories. These project operations remain explicit.
- No ClassicUA translations or generated TTS are included in the existing Stage 1 diagnostic addon. Stage 2A's metadata-only audit remains as designed.
- The older `docs/LEGAL_AND_ATTRIBUTION.md` and Stage 0 planning documents are historical snapshots written **before** this reply; their earlier "unresolved" wording is superseded for the requested ClassicUA translation use by this record. Preserve their pinned hashes rather than rewriting them.

## Engineering policy

1. Read ClassicUA through an explicitly pinned, clean source checkout with recorded hashes and provenance.
2. Preserve a verifiable mapping to source units and canonical Spoken IDs; document adaptations as appropriate, following the requested acknowledgement.
3. Distinguish approval to **use translated text** from technical readiness of a **runtime text-display contract** for any Spoken module.
4. Preserve review gates for fidelity, AI-marked translations, grammar templates, matching identity, stale narration, third-party lore and pack quality.
5. Publish only after an explicit release request and applicable compatibility, attribution and rights checks.

Do not interpret this permission as a reason to automatically commit the entire ClassicUA corpus or create direct, unreviewed translations/audio releases.
